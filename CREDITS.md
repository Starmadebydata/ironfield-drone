# Third-party assets

All models below were downloaded from **Poly Pizza** (https://poly.pizza) as
`.glb` and converted to `.fbx` with `tools/blender/convert_gltf.py`. The
original `.glb` files are kept under `tools/external_src/`. In-game they are
rescaled and fitted with gameplay parts by `Assets/Editor/IronfieldSetup.cs`.
